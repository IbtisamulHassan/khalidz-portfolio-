How I work

I learn by shipping real things. When AB Agro Mart — a fresh fish, meat, and grocery retailer — was getting ready to open, it needed the kind of operational infrastructure a bigger company would build a whole department for: task tracking, order management, a product catalog, and proper accounting. There was no off-the-shelf tool that fit, and no budget for one.

So I built it — sheet by sheet, script by script — learning Excel formulas, Python automation, and double-entry bookkeeping along the way, and testing every number against hand calculations until it held up.
