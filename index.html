How I work

From blank sheet to system the business runs on.

Step 01

Map the operation

Walk the whole business — orders, inventory, money, people — and write down every process that happens on paper or in someone's head.

Step 02

Design the spreadsheet architecture

Decide what lives in which sheet, how the sheets link, and where every number comes from — before writing a single formula.

Step 03

Build & verify by hand

Write the formulas and scripts, then test every total against hand calculations until the system agrees with reality — 11,000+ formulas, zero errors.

Step 04

Automate the repetitive parts

Turn anything done twice into a script: catalog rebuilds, monthly order sheets, order sync from the storefront into the tracker.

Step 05

Hand over documents people actually use

Policy pages, SOPs, and a bookkeeping system stress-tested with real transactions until assets equal liabilities plus equity.
